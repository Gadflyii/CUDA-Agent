# SM120a Muse Glimmer Groupwise-Q4 DFlash2 kernel campaign

Date: 2026-08-21. Host `Ron-9950X3D2`, NVIDIA GeForce RTX 5090, compute capability
12.0, driver 596.49, native Release `sm_120a` image, and CUDA 13.3.73. The campaign ran
only in the isolated `perf/agentic-kernels-sm120a-5090` worktree from accepted start
`dc688b12dfe8edf8c1ed50062f95b59fd648e262`.

The frozen production route was the registered
`muse-glimmer-30b/groupwise-int-dflash-q4` artifact with DFlash2 draft width 4,
INT8-G64 KV, stochastic sampling, 131,072-token capacity, and the current uncaptured
DFlash2 schedule. Candidate decisions used the maintained real Muse Program round at
8,192 resident tokens/request for C1 through C8 and C1 at 129,041 tokens. The method was
the active [kernel optimization campaign](../maintainer/kernel-optimization-campaign.md):
one falsifiable change at a time, independent correctness before timing, and two real
Program repetitions each at least 2% faster before a candidate could advance. The first
accepted candidate closed each cycle; a complete 24-candidate no-win cycle ended the
campaign.

## Terminal no-win cycle and dataset

Cycle 7 tested all 24 candidate slots and accepted none. Across the full campaign, 39
candidates produced six accepted and 33 rejected decisions. Each source candidate and
each restoration has its own Git commit. The committed
[machine-readable ledger](2026-08-21-sm120a-muse-kernel-campaign-ledger.csv) has one row
per candidate and 28 stable fields covering order, commits, hypothesis and dispatch
domain, compile/oracle/public-Op/Program/Engine gates, decision, restoration, and raw
evidence. Gates not reached are explicitly recorded as `not-run`; ignored Nsight reports,
timing CSVs, and diagnosis notes remain under
`profiles/sm120a/rtx5090/muse-glimmer-q4/cycle1` through `cycle7`.

The closest terminal candidate was cycle 7 candidate 24, an R32/C40/BK128 W8 vocabulary
head. It improved the isolated head 30.06% and the two C8 Program repetitions by 1.9643%
and 3.0023%. The first result missed the strict gate by 0.0357 percentage point, so the
candidate was rejected and restored rather than rounded into an acceptance.

## Ranked combination opportunities

No combination below was implemented or counted as a campaign candidate. These are
optimistic additive ceilings from separately measured rejected candidates, ranked for a
possible separately authorized consolidation campaign. Percentages use the 42.0805 ms
C8 baseline; they are not performance claims.

| Rank | Disjoint mechanisms | Optimistic additive saving | Interaction risk |
|---:|---|---:|---|
| 1 | candidate 24 R32/C40 W8 head + candidate 8 N19968 Q4 fragment ping-pong | 1.036-1.879 ms (2.46-4.46%) | Low dispatch overlap: W8 head and N19968 Q4 execute as separate kernels. Normal clock/L2 noise can still defeat simple addition. |
| 2 | candidate 24 W8 head + candidate 6 N19968 Q4 activation-L1 admission | 1.104-1.740 ms (2.62-4.14%) | Dispatches are distinct, but persistent L1 state from the Q4 route can interfere with the later W8 head; the additive model is weaker. |
| 3 | candidate 24 W8 head + candidate 11 selective R16 Q4 for N1024/N1664 | 1.023-1.460 ms (2.43-3.47%) | Lowest structural interference, but the 0.197 ms small-output contribution is a profile-derived ceiling and never reached a Program run. |
| 4 | candidate 24 W8 head + cycle-6 candidate 3 three-stage broad R32 Q4 | 1.170-1.607 ms (2.78-3.82%) | W8 and Q4 dispatches are separate, but the 0.344 ms Q4 result predates retained scale-pair staging in the same Q4 kernel. It must be remeasured on the final baseline. |
| 5 | candidate 21 R64/C40 W8 head + candidate 11 selective R16 Q4 | 0.280-1.227 ms (0.66-2.92%) | The exact-C40 head had one strong and one nearly neutral Program repetition; only its upper ceiling crosses the gate. |

The W8 C40 sweep makes candidate 24 the only credible head foundation: R64/C40 improved
the public Op 23.41% but Program repetitions varied from 0.20% to 2.45%; R48/C40 improved
the Op 28.71% but Program results ranged from a 0.44% regression to a 1.72% gain;
R32/C40 improved the Op 30.06% and produced the closest repeated Program result.

The additive ranges use the smaller and larger separately observed Program savings where
both were measured. Candidate 11 contributes only its 0.197 ms profiled production ceiling,
and cycle-6 candidate 3 contributes its 0.344 ms profiled ceiling. W8 alternatives are
mutually exclusive and must never be added to one another. N19968 launch-bound, cache, and
ping-pong alternatives also overlap one route and are mutually exclusive. Broad
three-stage Q4 and selective R16 partially overlap the small-output shapes, while the L1
cache variants can perturb neighboring kernels; those pairs have no defensible additive
ceiling.

## Verification and limitations

The final retained SM120a image passed the real Muse artifact test, load-plan test,
decoded-Q4 FP64 oracle, decoded-W8 oracle, grouped dynamic-convolution oracle, Muse GQA
oracle, DFlash2 selector oracle, a Program smoke round, the complete C1-C8/129K timing
matrix, and both Engine sentinels. The same retained source cross-compiled the CLI,
Program benchmark, Q4 oracle, and W8 oracle targets for `sm_86` and `sm_89` with CUDA
13.3; these builds are compile evidence only, not physical qualification.

The campaign establishes the retained performance only on the RTX 5090 `sm_120a` image,
the named artifact, DFlash2 k4, and INT8-G64 KV. It does not establish other draft widths,
KV formats, artifacts, captured DFlash2 execution, or physical SM86/SM89 performance. The
terminal no-win cycle restored the accepted source; no rejected candidate or speculative
combination remains enabled.
