# SM89 Muse and Qwen rerun campaign

Date: 2026-08-21. This campaign reruns Muse Glimmer first and Qwen3.8-27B second on the WSL host
`DESKTOP-DG588BU`, NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4090, compute capability 8.9, driver 596.21, CUDA 13.3.73,
and a Release `sm_89` image. It uses only the isolated worktree
`/ai/ginfer/.worktrees/agentic-kernels-sm89-rerun`, branch
`perf/agentic-kernels-sm89-rerun`, seeded at the exact accepted commit
`ec7c79679d466b0a427bd44d1a85ed78db729c73`. The primary checkout and the existing SM89 worktree
remain read-only.

## Frozen controls

- Target order: Muse Glimmer DFlash2, then Qwen3.8-27B MTP3.
- Candidate policy: one mechanism per ordinary candidate, maximum 24 candidates per cycle, and
  first accepted candidate closes and restarts from a fresh baseline/profile.
- Real-boundary gate: two independent matched repetitions must each achieve either at least 1.5%
  lower GPU round latency or at least +3.0 aggregate published tokens/s at the declared primary
  concurrency. The absolute arm also requires positive matched latency, a delta above fresh
  baseline noise, and identical drafted, accepted, and licensed-token accounting. Repetitions and
  concurrency points are not averaged to cross the gate.
- Correctness/neutrality: independent public-Op oracle, affected matrix and dispatch seams,
  unaffected concurrency controls, complete C=1..8 review, registered real-artifact test, and
  short/long public Engine semantic sentinels. Production prefill and Engine runs use
  `CUDA_MODULE_LOADING=EAGER`.
- Terminal rule: one complete 24-candidate single-mechanism no-win cycle freezes its ledger, then
  starts a separate maximum-24-package consolidation phase when compatible evidence-supported
  packages exist. The target stops after a complete consolidation no-win cycle, or immediately
  when no package has a plausible current-baseline path to the same gate.
- Evidence: durable summaries and ledgers are committed; bulk profiler reports and logs remain
  under ignored `profiles/sm89/rtx4090/` paths.
