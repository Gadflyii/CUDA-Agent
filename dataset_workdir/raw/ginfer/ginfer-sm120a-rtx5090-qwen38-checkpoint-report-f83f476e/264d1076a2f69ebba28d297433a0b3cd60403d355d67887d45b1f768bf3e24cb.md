# SM120a Qwen3.8 NVFP4 + Q4 DFlash2 kernel campaign

Date: 2026-08-21. Host `Ron-9950X3D2`, NVIDIA GeForce RTX 5090, compute capability 12.0,
driver 596.49, native Release `sm_120a` image, and CUDA 13.3.73. The campaign runs only in the
isolated `perf/agentic-kernels-sm120a-5090` worktree. Its integrated source boundary was
`f5292a8fbec5ee3990f6b7f10a9ba400546d51fc`; benchmark-enabling commit `fb544c44` added the
maintained Qwen DFlash2 Program round before candidate 1.

The frozen route is registered `qwen3.8-27b/nvfp4-dflash2-q4`: stochastic sampling, learned
DFlash2 width 4, adaptive target verification through 15, INT8-G64 KV, CUDA Graphs, and fixed
131,072-token context/capacity. Candidate decisions use 8,192 resident tokens/request at C1
through C8 and C1 at 129,041 tokens. The active
[kernel optimization campaign](../maintainer/kernel-optimization-campaign.md) governs the run:
one mechanism per candidate, independent correctness before timing, two affected production
repetitions, and the first accepted candidate closes a cycle. Cycles 1 and 2 used the frozen 2%
Program-latency gate recorded below. Effective with cycle 3, after correctness and no-regression
gates, each of two independent real-boundary repetitions must either reduce matched Program GPU
round latency by at least 1.5%, or improve published production throughput by at least 3.0 tok/s at
the declared primary concurrency. The throughput arm additionally requires a positive matched
round-latency change, a paired delta above fresh-baseline noise, and identical drafted, accepted,
and licensed accounting; neither repetitions nor concurrency points are averaged to cross a gate.
Raw Nsight reports and timing logs remain ignored under
`profiles/sm120a/rtx5090/qwen38-nvfp4-dflash2/`.
