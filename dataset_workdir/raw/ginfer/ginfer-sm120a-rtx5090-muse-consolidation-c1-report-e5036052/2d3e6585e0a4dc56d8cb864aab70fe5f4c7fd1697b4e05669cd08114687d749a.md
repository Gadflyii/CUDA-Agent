A fresh Nsight Systems 2025.5.2 capture of one post-warmup C8 round measured 44.43 ms of summed
kernel time. Pair32/BK128 Q4 accounted for 32.74 ms: 12.44 ms in the N19968 R64/C40 route and
20.30 ms in the R32/C40 families. The retained W8 vocabulary head accounted for 2.69 ms. Those
three sequential domains are the only initial combination components with separately measured
savings large enough to plausibly clear the 0.848 ms cycle gate.

Cycle 1 therefore begins with original candidate `c7_24` (R32/C40 W8 head) plus a disjoint Q4
component. The strongest first package pairs it with `c7_08` (N19968 fragment ping-pong): their
separately observed Program savings add to an optimistic 1.036-1.879 ms (2.46-4.46% of the old
42.0805 ms boundary). The kernels are sequential and dispatch-disjoint; shared L2 state,
power/clock behavior, and measurement noise are the expected non-additive risks.

## Cycle 1 result

Candidates 1-4 were positive at one or more lower gates but did not satisfy two independent 2%
Program repetitions. Candidate 5 combined original `c7_24` (R32/C40 W8 head), `c7_06` (N19968
activation-L1 admission), and `c7_11` (selective R16/C40 for N1024/N1664). These are three
dispatch-disjoint domains. The candidate retained all four public-Op gains and measured 41.5275
and 41.3232 ms at C8, improvements of 2.0566% and 2.5385% from the conservative 42.3995 ms
baseline. It therefore closed cycle 1.

The complete C1-C8/129K matrix was neutral or faster, all independent oracle and real-artifact
tests passed, and the 8K and 129,041-token Engine sentinels retrieved the exact ORCHID record with
DFlash2 k4 and INT8-G64 KV. The new C40 W8 selection is limited to the qualified `sm_120a` image;
the package also cross-compiles in the `sm_86` and `sm_89` images without changing their dispatch.
Candidate source commits are `a1d20707` and qualification-containment commit `19687307`.
