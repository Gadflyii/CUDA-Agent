- Host: `Ron-9950X3D2`; NVIDIA GeForce RTX 5090; compute capability 12.0; driver 596.49;
  native Release `sm_120a` image; CUDA 13.3.73.
- Artifact: `/ai/ginfer/.worktrees/muse-glimmer/out/muse_glimmer_30b_dflash_q4.ninfer`;

- Route: registered `muse-glimmer-30b/groupwise-int-dflash-q4`; DFlash2 draft width 4;
  stochastic sampling; INT8-G64 KV; fixed 131,072-token capacity; prefix reuse disabled;
  public graph option enabled while DFlash2 remains uncaptured.
- Real boundary: maintained Muse Program round at 8,192 resident tokens/request for C1 through
  C8, plus C1 at 129,041 tokens. Candidate decisions use C8 unless a package targets another
  measured point.
- Correctness: independent decoded-Q4 FP64 and decoded-W8 oracles, grouped dynamic-convolution
  oracle, Muse GQA oracle, DFlash2 selector oracle, load-plan test, real-artifact test, and short
  and long public Engine retrieval sentinels.
- Candidate: one coherent package derived from explicitly named rows in the immutable original
  ledger. The package records whether components are disjoint, overlapping, or cache/resource
  interacting, and predicts an additive ceiling before source mutation.
- Budget: at most 24 packages per cycle. The first package that clears every gate closes the
  cycle and becomes the next baseline. Stop after one complete 24-package cycle has no accepted
  result.
- Acceptance: affected public Ops and independent oracles pass; two independent real Program
  repetitions each improve the fresh baseline by at least 2%; the complete C1-C8/129K matrix,
  route seams, real test, and short/long Engine sentinels pass without material unsupported
  regression.
- History: every source package is committed before testing. A rejection receives a normal revert
  and one row in the distinct
  [consolidation ledger](2026-08-21-sm120a-muse-consolidation-ledger.csv). Bulk evidence remains
  ignored under `profiles/sm120a/rtx5090/muse-glimmer-q4-consolidation/`.

## Cycle 1 result

Candidates 1-4 were positive at one or more lower gates but did not satisfy two independent 2%
Program repetitions. Candidate 5 combined original `c7_24` (R32/C40 W8 head), `c7_06` (N19968
activation-L1 admission), and `c7_11` (selective R16/C40 for N1024/N1664). These are three
dispatch-disjoint domains. The candidate retained all four public-Op gains and measured 41.5275
and 41.3232 ms at C8, improvements of 2.0566% and 2.5385% from the conservative 42.3995 ms
baseline. It therefore closed cycle 1.

The complete C1-C8/129K matrix was neutral or faster, all independent oracle and real-artifact
tests passed, and the 8K and 129,041-token Engine sentinels retrieved the exact ORCHID record with
DFlash2 k4 and INT8-G64 KV. The new C40 W8 selection is limited to the qualified `sm_120a` image;
the package also cross-compiles in the `sm_86` and `sm_89` images without changing their dispatch.
Candidate source commits are `a1d20707` and qualification-containment commit `19687307`.

## Cycle 2 terminal result

All 24 authorized packages were committed, tested through the narrow-to-real gate appropriate to
their evidence, recorded, and reverted when rejected. No package was accepted, so this full no-win
cycle terminates the consolidation campaign under the frozen rule. The retained implementation is
exactly the accepted cycle-1 source; there is no source difference from `e5036052` under `src/`,
`include/`, `tests/`, `bench/`, or the root build definition.

The first seven cycle-2 packages were built without explicitly naming the standalone public
benchmark in the incremental build target, leaving the benchmark executable's relink provenance
ambiguous. Candidates 8-14 applied the exact same seven source patches (verified by stable Git
patch ID), explicitly rebuilt `ginfer_linear_bench`, and independently reproduced the decisions.
The immutable candidate rows remain intact; the machine-readable
[correction map](2026-08-21-sm120a-muse-consolidation-corrections.csv) identifies every affected
row, its exact validating row, the corrected measurement, and the unchanged decision.

The strongest measured combination directions after provenance correction rank as follows:

Further W8-head combinations do not supply the missing saving. BK64 regressed the retained W8
head from 1004.864 to 1508.640 us, and relaxing its four-block bound regressed it to 1051.904 us.
The alternative N19968 geometries (twenty warps, R48, R96/BK64, and R128/BK64), scalar-scale
loads, dual-cache admission, and the complete latency package all materially regressed their public
domain. Those mechanisms overlap occupancy, L1, or issue resources rather than contributing a
disjoint additive saving. No remaining measured combination has a defensible additive ceiling at
or above 0.812 ms.
