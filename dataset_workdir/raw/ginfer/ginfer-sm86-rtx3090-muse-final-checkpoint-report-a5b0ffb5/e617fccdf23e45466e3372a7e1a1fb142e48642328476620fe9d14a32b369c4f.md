# SM86 agentic kernel campaign: Muse Glimmer

Date: 2026-08-21. Host `AIS-1-2950X-L02`, NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3090, compute capability
8.6, driver 610.57.04, Release `sm_86` image, CUDA 13.3.73. The campaign runs in the
isolated `perf/agentic-kernels` worktree; the primary checkout remains untouched.

The frozen route is `muse-glimmer-30b/groupwise-int`, DFlash2 draft width 4, INT8-G64
Main KV, a fixed 131,072-token capacity, and the artifact
`/mnt/nas/AltaStratusAI/models/Muse-Glimmer-30B-NInfer/muse_glimmer_30b.ninfer`
(18,047,450,368 bytes). Program and Engine measurements use
`CUDA_MODULE_LOADING=EAGER`. The campaign control is at most 24 candidates per cycle;
the first fully qualified candidate starts a fresh cycle, and one complete 24-candidate
no-win cycle enters the prescribed consolidation terminal. Each of two independent
real-round repetitions qualifies through either at least 1.5% matched GPU round-latency
reduction or at least 3.0 aggregate published tok/s at the declared primary concurrency.
The throughput arm also requires positive latency, a paired delta clearly above fresh-
baseline noise, and identical drafted, accepted, and licensed-token accounting. Public
Op, route-seam/control, independent-oracle, Engine-sentinel, and registered-real-test
gates remain mandatory.
